# Influence of AP Particle Size on the Burning Rate of HTPB/AP/Al Composite Propellants

## Abstract

This study investigates the effect of ammonium perchlorate (AP) particle size on the burning rate of hydroxyl-terminated polybutadiene (HTPB) based composite propellants containing aluminum (Al) powder. The burning rate increased significantly when AP particle size decreased from 200 μm to 20 μm.

## 1. Introduction

Composite solid propellants based on HTPB binder with AP oxidizer and Al fuel are widely used in rocket motors. The density of AP is approximately 1.95 g/cm³, and it constitutes about 65-70% of the propellant by mass.

RDX (Cyclotrimethylenetrinitramine) is another important energetic compound with a density of 1.82 g/cm³ and a detonation velocity of 8750 m/s.

HMX (Octogen) has a density of 1.91 g/cm³ and a detonation velocity of 9100 m/s, making it one of the most powerful military explosives.

## 2. Experimental

### 2.1 Materials

The following materials were used in this study:
- HTPB binder (hydroxyl value: 0.82 mmol/g)
- Ammonium perchlorate (AP) with particle sizes of 20 μm, 50 μm, 100 μm, and 200 μm
- Aluminum powder (Al) with particle size of 20 μm
- IPDI (isophorone diisocyanate) as curing agent

### 2.2 Formulation

The base formulation consisted of:
- HTPB: 12 wt%
- AP: 68 wt%
- Al: 18 wt%
- IPDI: 2 wt%

The curing temperature was maintained at 60°C for 7 days.

### 2.3 Burning Rate Measurement

Burning rate measurements were conducted in a Crawford bomb at pressures ranging from 3 MPa to 10 MPa.

## 3. Results and Discussion

### 3.1 Burning Rate

The burning rate results are presented in Table 1.

| AP Particle Size (μm) | Burning Rate at 7 MPa (mm/s) | Pressure Exponent |
|----------------------|------------------------------|-------------------|
| 200                  | 5.2                          | 0.35              |
| 100                  | 6.8                          | 0.38              |
| 50                   | 8.5                          | 0.41              |
| 20                   | 11.3                         | 0.45              |

The burning rate of the HTPB/AP/Al propellant with 20 μm AP particle size reached 8.5 mm/s at 7 MPa pressure.

### 3.2 Mechanical Properties

The tensile strength of the cured propellant was measured at 0.85 MPa, with an elongation at break of 42%.

## 4. Thermochemical Properties

The heat of explosion of the HTPB/AP/Al propellant was measured as 5420 kJ/kg.

The oxygen balance of AP is -34.04%.

TNT (trinitrotoluene) has a density of 1.65 g/cm³ and a detonation velocity of 6900 m/s. The heat of explosion of TNT is 4520 kJ/kg.

TATB (triaminotrinitrobenzene) has a density of 1.94 g/cm³ and is known for its insensitivity. The detonation velocity of TATB is 7350 m/s.

## 5. Conclusions

1. The burning rate of HTPB/AP/Al composite propellants increases with decreasing AP particle size.
2. At 7 MPa, reducing AP particle size from 200 μm to 20 μm increased the burning rate from 5.2 to 11.3 mm/s.
3. The pressure exponent also increased slightly with smaller AP particle sizes.
